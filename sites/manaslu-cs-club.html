Who we are

A community built around curiosity.

Featured project · Prototype concept

Meet GYRO-76

A robot prototype concept exploring how robotics and sustainability can come together. GYRO-76 represents the club's interest in thoughtful engineering, material reuse, and building with purpose.

GYRO-76 // CONCEPT VISUAL

GYRO-76 / ROBOT PROTOTYPE

A concept-led prototype focused on combining accessible engineering with sustainable material choices.

Design with purpose

Build smarter. Waste less.

GYRO-76 highlights an approach to robotics that considers not only what a machine can do, but also how it is made. The project explores the use of sustainable or reused materials where practical, alongside iterative prototyping and hands-on technical learning.

Sustainable materialsRoboticsPrototype developmentHands-on learning
Project direction

GYRO-76 is presented here as a robot prototype initiative. The concept emphasizes evaluating reusable materials for suitable structural components, considering durability and repairability, and improving the design through testing and iteration.
